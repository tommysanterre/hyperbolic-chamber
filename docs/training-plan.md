# Training Plan

Living document maintained together with [equipment-inventory.md](equipment-inventory.md) and [training-log.md](training-log.md).

## Athlete Profile & Priorities

- **Primary goals:** Living pain-free, functional strength, joint resilience, and general cardiovascular fitness.
- **Time budget:** Strict **3 × 30 minutes/week** of dedicated workouts (2× full-body strength + 1× cardio), plus optional low-fatigue daily movement snacks.
- **Cardio baseline:** Starting from zero structured cardio.
- **Pain-free progression rules:**
  - Always stop with **1–2 clean reps left in reserve**; never grind ugly reps or push through sharp/joint pain.
  - Use **double progression**: stay at a given load/leverage until you hit the top of the rep/time range cleanly across all 3 sets, then make one small progression (lower incline height, straighten legs, lighter band, add 5 lb per dumbbell) and restart at the bottom of the range.
  - Keep freestanding Corength parallel bars loaded vertically so feet stay planted on the EVA foam tiles.

## Active 3 × 30-Minute Weekly Program (Owned Gear Only)

Suggested cadence: **Mon (Strength A) / Wed (Cardio) / Fri (Strength B)**, with at least one rest day between strength sessions.

### Day 1 — Strength A (30 min)

- **Warm-up (4 min):** Light mobility (arm circles, hip hinges, 5 bodyweight squats, 10 light band pull-aparts with DYNASQUARE Red, 3–5 easy scapular pull-ups) + 1 easy ramp-up set of Pair 1.
- **Main work (24 min):** 3 antagonist supersets × 3 rounds each (~45s rest after #1, ~60–75s after #2).
- **Cooldown (2 min):** 20–30s relaxed **Dead hang**.

| Pair | Movement (Active — Owned Gear) | Sets × Target | Setup & Progression Cues | Wishlist Upgrade (Once Owned) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A1** | **Tempo Bodyweight Squat** | 3 × 8–12 | 3-second controlled lower, pain-free depth, tall torso | **Goblet Squat** (NÜOBELL) |
| **A2** | **Incline Push-ups** | 3 × 6–12 | Corength bar (`93 → 86 → 79 → 72 cm`, force vertical/braced on foam) → sturdy ~45 cm step or knee push-ups on Bitor parallettes → full push-ups on Bitor parallettes | Same (or DB Floor Press) |
| **B1** | **Parallel Bar Inverted Rows** | 3 × 8–12 | One hand on each Corength bar (`86–93 cm`), neutral grip, body between bars; pull straight up. Progress: knees bent → straight legs on heels | Keep, or alternate with **Supported One-Arm DB Row** (hand on Corength bar) |
| **B2** | **Heel-Dig Bridges** | 3 × 8–12 | Supine, heels planted, toes up, hips to straight line without low-back arch; walk heels farther out to progress | **DB Glute Bridge** (or keep Heel-Dig Bridges) |
| **C1** | **Band Pull-Aparts** | 3 × 12–15 | **DYNASQUARE Red (10–35 lb)**, hold a **single strand**, shoulders down, ribs tucked, palms up or neutral; narrow grip to progress | Keep |
| **C2** | **Kneeling Ab Wheel Rollouts** | 3 × 5–8 | Short controlled range, ribs tucked, zero lower-back sag (swap to Bear Plank Hold if low back feels stressed) | **DB Suitcase Carry** (3 × 20–30s / side) |

### Day 2 — Cardio (30 min)

- **Active (Owned Gear / No Machine Yet):** 30-minute **Zone 2** brisk walk, uphill/incline walk, light ruck (backpack), or cycling.
  - **5 min** easy warm-up → **20 min** steady conversational pace (short sentences possible) → **5 min** easy cooldown + hip-flexor stretch.
- **Wishlist Upgrade (Rowing Machine):**
  - **Weeks 1–4:** 5 min warm-up + 20 min steady Zone 2 (damper **3–5**, ~18–20 strokes/min) + 5 min cooldown.
  - **Week 5+:** Alternate weekly between **25 min steady Zone 2** and **4 × (3 min brisk + 2 min very easy)** with 5 min warm-up and cooldown. Also use 2–3 min easy rowing as the Strength A/B warm-up.

### Day 3 — Strength B (30 min)

- **Warm-up (4 min):** Light hip hinges, 10 light band pull-aparts (DYNASQUARE Red), 5 bodyweight squats + 1 easy ramp-up set of Pair 1.
- **Main work (24 min):** 3 supersets × 3 rounds each (~45s rest after #1, ~60–75s after #2).
- **Cooldown (2 min):** 20–30s **Dead hang** + brief hip-flexor stretch.

| Pair | Movement (Active — Owned Gear) | Sets × Target | Setup & Progression Cues | Wishlist Upgrade (Once Owned) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A1** | **Supported Single-Leg RDL** | 3 × 6–10 / side | One hand on sturdy support, soft standing knee, hips level, long back | **Dumbbell RDL** (3 × 8–12) |
| **A2** | **Bear Plank Shoulder Taps** | 3 × 6–10 / side | Hands on Bitor parallettes or floor, knees hovering slightly, hips quiet without rocking | **Neutral-Grip DB Overhead Press** (palms facing each other, standing or half-kneeling, 3 × 8–12) |
| **B1** | **Band-Assisted Chin-ups** | 3 × 4–8 | **GoZone Orange (50–60 lb)** looped over pull-up bar (knee or foot in band; stack with **DYNASQUARE Red** for ~60–95 lb assist if needed). Smooth 2–3s lower. Progress: Orange + Red → Orange → Red → Unassisted | Keep (progress toward unassisted chin-ups) |
| **B2** | **Bodyweight Reverse Lunges** | 3 × 6–10 / side | Step backward (gentler on knees than forward lunges), upright torso | **DB Reverse Lunges** (goblet or suitcase hold) |
| **C1** | **Tuck L-Sit** | 3 × 10–20s | Tall Corength parallel bars, straight arms, shoulders depressed; progress to low Bitor parallettes later | Same |
| **C2** | **Bear Plank Hold** | 3 × 15–25s | Knees hovering 2–3 cm under hips, level back, wrists neutral on Bitor parallettes if preferred | **Kneeling Ab Wheel Rollouts** (3 × 5–8) once Suitcase Carries move into Strength A |

## Daily Movement Snack Loop (8 Movements — Owned Gear Only)

Role: **Low-fatigue daily mobility, posture, and joint lubrication** (RPE 4–5, never near failure, zero soreness). Spread **1–2 snacks at a time** across the day—primarily on the **4 non-workout days** (on workout days, skip snacks or keep them strictly to mobility resets well away from your 30-min session).

1. **Dead hang** — `20–30 seconds` *(pull-up bar; shoulder decompression & grip)*
2. **Deep squat hold** — `20–30 seconds` *(floor; hip, ankle & lower-back mobility)*
3. **Band pull-aparts** — `10–15 reps` *(DYNASQUARE Red 10–35 lb, single strand; upper-back posture & shoulder health)*
4. **Three-point bridge** — `3–5 reps per side` *(floor; thoracic rotation & hip opening)*
5. **Scapular pull-ups** — `3–5 reps` *(pull-up bar; easy scapular control)*
6. **Hip-flexor stretch** — `20–30 seconds per side` *(floor; counters prolonged sitting)*
7. **Squats** — `10 reps` *(floor; easy knee lubrication & blood flow)*
8. **Bear plank hold** — `10–15 seconds` *(Bitor parallettes or floor; gentle core & shoulder stability)*
